1 · Three mark directions

Each tested as an app icon at 32 px and 16 px, and as a bare mark at 16 px
Direction 1Recommended
Interlocking links

Two rounded links pass through each other, over and under. Separate parts, one system — without a chain or a padlock.

Holds up at 16 px
Direction 2Explored
Slot-in tiles

Three parts in place, the fourth sliding home to complete the set. Friendly and very legible, but close to generic app-grid icons.

Offset is lost at 16 px
Direction 3Explored
Keyed i

The dot’s tab keys into a notch in the stem, with a 2 px gap. Reads as the name’s first letter; the lock only shows at large sizes.

Key detail vanishes small
Recommendation: Interlocking links. It says “interlock” at a glance, stays distinctive at 512 px and legible at 16 px, and avoids chains, puzzle pieces and padlocks.
