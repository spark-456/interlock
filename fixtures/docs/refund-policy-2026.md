# Larkspur refund policy (2026)

Dated 2 January 2026. This policy replaces every earlier refund page.

## 1. Who can ask for a refund
Any customer on a monthly or annual plan, for invoices issued to their own account.

## 2. Refunds on subscriptions
Customers can request a full refund within 30 days of the invoice date for any monthly or annual plan.
Refunds above $100 are reviewed by a person before they are paid.

## 3. How refunds are paid
Refunds go back to the original payment method within 5 to 10 business days.

## 4. What is not refunded
Usage charges for messages already sent, and invoices older than 30 days.
