# Plans and limits

| Plan    | Price          | Seats | Messages per month |
|---------|----------------|-------|--------------------|
| Starter | $12 per month  | 1     | 2,000              |
| Team    | $48 per month  | 10    | 25,000             |
| Scale   | $240 per month | 50    | 250,000            |

You can change plans at any time. Moving up takes effect straight away and is prorated.
Moving down takes effect at the next renewal. Refunds follow the refund policy.
Support responds within one business day on Starter and within four hours on Team and Scale.
